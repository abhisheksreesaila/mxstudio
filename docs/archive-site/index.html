Safe to share. One key per app, HTTPS on your LAN.

Keys are shown once and stored only as hashes; revoke one and only that app stops. mxc expose puts the endpoint on your network over HTTPS.

Why MAX?

max serveOpenAI-compatibleModular's porting skillMAX 26.7 nightly

MAX already serves models well. omxconsole is the convenience around it: one endpoint, idle unload, keys, a console.

Why not Ollama or vLLM?

use themif you want their engines.
use mxcif you want MAX, served that easily, and a way to port what it can't run.

Does it need the cloud?
